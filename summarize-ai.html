How it works

TF-IDF Scoring

Every word is scored by how often it appears in this document vs. how common it is generally — surfacing what's truly important.

Sentence Ranking

Each sentence is ranked by the total importance score of its words. The top-scoring sentences become your summary.

Order Preserved

Selected sentences are returned in their original order so the summary reads naturally, just like the source text.
